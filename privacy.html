Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version: PupTalk keeps your data on your device. We have no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and we do not receive your recordings, photos or dog's details.

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the PupTalk – Dog Translator app for iPhone ("PupTalk", "the app", "we") handles your information.

Information stored on your device

You choose what to add to the app. The following stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Audio recordings: your dog's barks and your voice, recorded when you use the translator.
  • Dog profiles: name, breed, age and any photo you choose.
  • Translation history: results, moods and dates. The app keeps your latest 300 translations and removes older ones along with their recordings.
  • Care and health details: reminders, vaccine, vet and medication entries, notes, due dates and weight.
  • App activity and settings: completed lessons, training days, daily translation count and preferences.

We do not have access to any of this information because the app does not send it to us or to any server.

Device permissions

  • Microphone: used only to record barks and your voice for translations. Audio is analyzed on your device.
  • Photos: you pick a photo through the iOS photo picker, and the app receives only that photo. It is resized and saved in the app. If you save a translation card, it is added to your photo library only when you choose to.
  • Notifications: used for care reminders that you set. These are scheduled on your device.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Some features will not work without them.

On-device analysis

Mood results use the pitch, rhythm and loudness of a recording along with Apple's built-in sound classifier. Breed suggestions and photo framing use Apple's Vision framework. All of this processing happens on your device.

Information we may receive

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to reply and help you. Please do not send sensitive details we do not need.

Third parties

PupTalk does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and we do not sell your information. Some services are provided outside the app:

  • Apple: if purchases are offered, Apple processes payments, subscriptions and restores. We do not receive your payment details. App Store downloads, ratings and iCloud or device backups are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Sharing: when you share a translation card or text, it goes to the app or person you select, and their privacy practices apply.
  • External links: media credit links open websites we do not control.

Backups

Your app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups of your device, depending on your device settings.

Deleting your information

  • Delete a single translation, or clear a dog's history, in the History screen. This also deletes the related recordings.
  • Delete a dog to remove its profile, photo, translations, recordings, reminders, health records, weights and lesson progress.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device. Copies in existing backups follow your backup settings.

Deleted data cannot be recovered by us, because we never hold a copy.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information. Most of these can be done directly in the app. For support emails we hold, contact us and we will help with your request.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's security features, such as your passcode and iOS app storage protections. No system is completely secure, so please keep your device locked and up to date.

Children

PupTalk is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
